Spoolify imports only Extended Streaming History JSON. If the ZIP is an account-info export, validation will stop before import.

How do I get this?
  1. Sign in at spotify.com and go to Account → Privacy settings.
  2. Scroll to Download your data.
  3. Choose Extended streaming history — not "Account data". The extended history contains your full play-by-play timeline across all years.
  4. Click Request data. Spotify will send you an email when the archive is ready.
  5. This can take up to 30 days. Spotify prepares the full history archive in batches — you will not get a download right away.
  6. When the email arrives, download the ZIP and save it to your device.

The ZIP contains JSON files named like Streaming_History_Audio_YYYY_N.json. These are the only files Spoolify imports — all other archive contents are ignored.

            Optional: Set up Spotify sync (for near-real-time updates)
            
Your imported archive is your historical foundation. If you want Spoolify to stay fresh by pulling recent plays from Spotify automatically, you will need a free Spotify Developer app. This is optional — archive import works fully without it.

            
Step 1 — Create a Spotify Developer app

            
    
              
  1. Go to developer.spotify.com and sign in with your Spotify account.
    2. 
              
  2. Click Create app. Give it any name (e.g. "Spoolify Local") and a brief description.
    3. 
              
  3. Under Redirect URIs, add exactly: http://localhost:8888/callback
    4. 
              
  4. Set the app type to Web API. Save the app.
    5. 
              
  5. Open the app settings and note your Client ID and Client Secret.
    6. 
            

            
Step 2 — Add credentials to your local config

            
Copy .env_example to .env and fill in the following keys:

            
SPOTIFY_CLIENT_ID=your_client_id_here
SPOTIFY_CLIENT_SECRET=your_client_secret_here
SPOTIFY_REDIRECT_URI=http://localhost:8888/callback

            
Required scopes

            
When sync is set up, Spoolify will request these Spotify permissions: user-read-recently-played and user-read-currently-playing. No other scopes are required.

            
Your credentials stay in your local .env file and are never sent anywhere by Spoolify. Sync is a future capability — configuring this now just means you will be ready when it is available.
